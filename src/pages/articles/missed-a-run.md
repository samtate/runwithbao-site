Every training plan looks perfect on day one. Then work runs late, a cold arrives, or a weekend away eats your long run. Missing a run is normal. What matters is what you do next.

## Don't try to catch up

The most common mistake is doubling up: running Tuesday's session on Wednesday *and* Wednesday's too. Two hard days in a row is how niggles start. A missed easy run is nearly always best left missed. A missed key session (the long run or the week's hardest one) is worth moving, if there's room.

## Move it, a little

If you can move a run a day or two without landing next to another hard session, do. Keep at least one easy day or rest day between hard efforts. Moving a run more than three days usually means it's really next week's problem, so let it go.

**In Bao:** hold a run on the calendar and drag it up to three days either way. It still counts towards the week it came from, so a busy week doesn't leave the next one overloaded.

## Ill? Use the "above the neck" rule

A common rule of thumb: if it's just a head cold (runny nose, mild sore throat), an easy, shorter run is usually fine. If it's below the neck (chest, fever, aching muscles, an upset stomach), rest. When in doubt, rest, and check with your GP if you're unsure. Come back gently: a few easy days before anything hard.

**In Bao:** tell Bao you were ill or injured, even for days that have already passed, and your streak carries on. Only a run you choose to let go breaks it.

## Plan holidays as holidays

A week off doesn't undo months of running. If you know you're away, plan it as time off rather than skipping runs one by one; it's better for your head as well as your legs. Coming back, ease in: a lighter first week, then back to normal.

**In Bao:** add time off from the Plan tab. The runs in between come off, your streak waits, and after a week or more Bao offers a gentle way back in.

## Tired? Do less, not nothing

A short, easy run beats a skipped one on a tough day. If you slept badly or played sport yesterday, swap the hard session for something gentle.

**In Bao:** tap **Make it easier** on any session for a shorter, easy version, and put the original back if you feel better. If you share your sleep and heart rate, Bao suggests it for you on days you look tired, and counts your other sport too.

## The real secret: consistency over perfection

Runners who improve aren't the ones who never miss a run. They're the ones who keep coming back. A plan that bends with your week is one you'll stick with.
